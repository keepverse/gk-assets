## How to attribute

If you use any of these assets, in whole or in part, please credit:

> **Artwork by [Keepverse](https://github.com/keepverse/gk-assets)**, licensed
> under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Any reasonable form of attribution works. A repo link plus the licence name and
a link to the licence satisfies it; a `NOTICE` or credits section in your
project, a comment in a config file, or a line in your game's credits screen all
count.

## What you may do

- Use the assets in a game, app, film, stream, or anything else.
- Modify and adapt them.
- Use them **commercially**, including in a paid product.
- Combine them with assets from other sources, as long as those are compatible.

## The one condition

**Give appropriate credit**, link to the licence, and **indicate whether you
made changes**. That is the whole of CC BY 4.0.

## Not covered by the licence

The Python tooling under `tools/` is **MIT-licensed, not CC BY** — see
[LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE). Creative Commons advises against applying CC BY to
source code, because its attribution condition does not work with the licences
open-source software is normally distributed under.

## Provenance

Every asset here is generated by the tooling in `tools/`, either procedurally
from noise and gradients or by rendering a Blender scene authored in this
repository. `vfx/<id>/` documents each effect's layers, frame timing, measured
values, and known issues.

**Licensing a new asset requires that it be one you have the right to license.**
Do not add artwork copied from another game, another artist's pack, or a
third-party asset store without checking that its licence is compatible and
attributable. If a file's provenance is unclear, it does not enter this
repository until it is clear.
